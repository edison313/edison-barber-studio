Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Information we collect

When you book an appointment with Edison Barber Studio, we collect the information you give us on the booking form:

  • Your name
  • Your mobile phone number
  • The service(s) you choose and the date and time of your appointment

We do not collect payment card information through this website, and we do not ask for sensitive personal information such as Social Security numbers.

How we use your information

  • To schedule, manage and confirm your appointment
  • To send you text messages (SMS) about your appointment, including a booking confirmation and a reminder about 1 hour before your appointment
  • To contact you if there is a change or problem with your appointment

SMS / text messaging

By entering your mobile phone number on our booking form and submitting a booking, you agree to receive appointment-related text messages from Edison Barber Studio at the number you provided. Message frequency varies (normally 1–3 messages per appointment). Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out of text messages; reply HELP for assistance. Opting out of text messages does not cancel your appointment — please call us at 646-379-8816 to change or cancel.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Sharing of information

We share your information only with the service providers we need to run the booking system and send appointment text messages (for example, our SMS messaging provider), and only so they can provide those services. We may disclose information if required by law.

Data retention and security

We keep booking records for as long as needed to provide our services and for our business records. We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no system is completely secure.

Your choices

You may ask us to correct or delete your personal information, or opt out of text messages at any time, by calling 646-379-8816 or replying STOP to any message.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version will always be posted on this page.
